# Store listing - English (en-US)

**Title (≤30):** Precord: Audio Replay Recorder

**Short description (≤80):** Always-on audio buffer. Save what you just heard - after it happened.

**Full description (≤4000):**

Never miss the moment again. Precord quietly keeps the last few minutes of sound in your phone's memory - and the second something important happens, one tap saves it. Like ShadowPlay or a dashcam, but for audio.

HOW IT WORKS
• Arm the buffer: 30 seconds up to 60 minutes (or any custom length).
• Something happens - tap SAVE REPLAY. The audio from BEFORE the tap is written to a file.
• Recording never fills your storage: old audio is overwritten in a RAM ring buffer until you save.

SAVE WITHOUT OPENING THE APP
• Button in the notification (works on the lock screen)
• Quick Settings tile to arm/disarm
• Shake your phone three times (optional)

BUILT FOR REAL AUDIO
• Engine written in Rust: lock-free ring buffer, no dropouts while saving
• 16 / 44.1 / 48 kHz, lossless WAV, M4A (AAC) or Opus
• Auto-normalise quiet, distant voices when saving
• Input gain, noise gate, raw-microphone mode
• Exact RAM estimate for every setting

CLIP LIBRARY & EDITOR
• Timestamped clips with titles and tags, search
• Waveform with tap-to-seek and drag-to-trim
• One-tap share, export to Recordings/Precord

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics. The app has no internet permission - your audio never leaves your device.

Industrial, minimal design inspired by classic hardware recorders. English, Русский, Українська.

Record only where the law and consent allow it.

**What's new (≤500):** Russian and Ukrainian translations, adaptive tablet layout, library search, tap-to-seek, raw-microphone mode, lower battery use when the screen is off.
